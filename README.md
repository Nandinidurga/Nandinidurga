## 🤖 About Me

I am a Bachelor of Technology student specializing in **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** at the Vasireddy Venkatadri Institute of Technology (9.2 CGPA). My technical journey thrives at the intersection of building intelligent deep-learning frameworks and optimizing tasks with accelerated processing architectures.

- 🚀 **HPC Experience:** Former Technical Intern at the **Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC), Pune**, where I engineered high-performance storage and retrieval workflows for massive telecom traffic datasets using Berkeley DB.

- 🧩 **Competitive Programmer:** Passionate problem solver with over **570+ problems solved on CodeChef** and active participation in competitive algorithm contests.
- 💡 **Hackathon Enthusiast:** Participated in the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH)**, building collaborative digital strategies focused on empowering agricultural ecosystems.
